# Headcount & People Cost Forecast — November 2026
**Entity:** LuminaUS | **Snapshot Date:** November 30, 2026 | **Period:** Nov-26 | **Prepared by:** Headcount Forecast Agent v0.1.0

## Executive Summary

LuminaUS closed November with **77 active employees** against a budget of **76**, a net headcount variance of +1. That headline masks two offsetting issues that require management attention before year-end.

**Engineering** is 5 heads over budget — a cost run-rate of **+$220,000 per month (+15.7%)** against plan. The over-plan hires began in August and have compounded each month. The full-year Engineering cost overrun is projected at **+$860,000 unfavorable**, the single largest driver of the FY2026 people cost variance.

**Content** is 3 heads under budget following three voluntary departures in November. The department's monthly attrition rate of **31.6%** is seventeen times the 2% trigger threshold and requires immediate HR attention. The cost underrun ($170,000/month, $340,000 full-year) is not a savings — it represents lost capacity and backfill cost risk heading into Q1 2027.

On a consolidated basis, FY2026 people costs are projected to land at **$51,270,000**, or **+$270,000 (+0.5%) above the $51,000,000 budget**. Engineering overspend is partially offset by favorable variances in Sales (under-plan headcount) and Content (attrition-driven underspend). Three Engineering open roles totaling **$720,000 in annual budget exposure** remain unfilled 46–90 days past their target start dates.

## Department Findings

### Engineering — INVESTIGATE

| Metric | Oct-26 | Nov-26 | Budget | Variance |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|----------|
| Headcount | 29 | 30 | 25 | **+5** |
| Fully Loaded Cost | $1,570,000 | $1,620,000 | $1,400,000 | **+$220,000 (+15.7%)** |

**MoM bridge:** One new hire (L2 Software Engineer, start Nov 1) brought Engineering from 29 to 30. No departures. The cumulative over-budget build began in August (26 actual) and has grown by one head each month through November.

**Root cause:** Five employees flagged in the roster (EMP-1026 through EMP-1030) carry notes indicating they were hired above approved headcount plan. All five are L2 or L3 Software Engineers with start dates between August and November 2026. The budget authorized 25 Engineering heads for all of FY2026; actual ran at 25 through June, then climbed to 30.

**Cost impact:** At the budget-implied average fully loaded cost of $56,000/month per head, five over-plan heads represent approximately $280,000/month in incremental run-rate cost. The budget sheet records the November variance at $220,000. The discrepancy suggests some of the over-plan hires carry lower-than-average fully loaded costs; use the budget sheet figure of **+$220,000** as the authoritative variance.

**Full-year:** Engineering will finish FY2026 at approximately **$17,660,000**, or **+$860,000 above the $16,800,000 budget**.

**Open pipeline risk (Engineering):** Three requisitions are past their target start date and remain unfilled:

| Req | Level | Days Past Target | Status | Annual FL Budget Exposure |
|-----|-------|-----------------|--------|--------------------------|
| REQ-101 | L2 Software Engineer | 90 days | Interviewing | $222,500 |
| REQ-102 | L3 Sr Software Engineer | 61 days | Offer Extended | $275,000 |
| REQ-103 | L2 Software Engineer | 46 days | Sourcing | $220,000 |
| **Total** | | | | **$717,500** |

These roles represent capacity commitments budgeted by the Engineering team for H2 2026. Until they are filled, the Engineering team is running below planned capacity even while over on headcount relative to the annual budget. REQ-102 (Offer Extended) is the closest to resolution; FP&A should model a December start for that role.

**Proposed action — FP&A Manager / Controller:** Confirm whether the five over-plan Engineering hires were approved via a formal budget exception or represent a headcount control failure. If exception approvals exist, update the budget baseline for FY2027 planning. If not approved, escalate to CFO for remediation guidance.
